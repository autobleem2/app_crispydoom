Doom (Shareware)
================

The first episode of Doom (id Software, 1993) - "Knee-Deep in the Dead", nine levels - played through
Crispy Doom, a faithful source port with a higher resolution and many optional fixes. The shareware
DOOM1.WAD is included. To play the full game, put your own DOOM.WAD in this folder and change
"-iwad DOOM1.WAD" in app.ini to "-iwad DOOM.WAD".


Controls
--------

D-Pad / left stick   Move forward and back, turn
Right stick          Strafe (on a pad that has one)
Cross                Fire
Circle               Use (open doors, press switches)
Square               Run (hold)
Triangle             Jump - switch "Allow jumping" on first, in Options -> Crispness
L1 / R1              Strafe left / right
L2 / R2              Previous / next weapon
Start                Menu
Select               Automap

To leave the game: press Reset on the console, or hold Start + Select (the console, the Pi and the PC
stick). On Windows: Start, then Quit Game.

Settings are kept in default.cfg and crispy-doom.cfg, the saved games in savegames/ - all in this folder.
An update of the App from the Store brings its own default.cfg and crispy-doom.cfg and so resets the
settings; the saved games are kept.
